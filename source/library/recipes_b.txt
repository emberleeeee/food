#ING

1 bunch kale, stems removed, chopped
3 cloves garlic, sliced
Pinch of chili flakes
2 cups Lentil and Roasted Carrot Salad

@@ Spiced Black Bean & Edamame Power Bowl (3-Day Batch) | Dinner, Lunch, Meal | fave | | 3 | 20 min | 520,33,,
#ING
2 1/4 cups canned black beans, rinsed and drained
2 1/4 cups frozen shelled edamame, thawed
3 cups green cabbage, finely shredded
6 tbsp lime juice (about 3 limes)
3 tbsp olive oil
1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
3/4 tsp garlic powder
3/4 tsp salt
6 tbsp cotija cheese, crumbled
1 1/2 avocados (slice day-of)
3 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
#STEP
Whisk the lime juice, olive oil, cumin, garlic powder and salt. It should taste bright and punchy.
Toss the black beans with a third of the dressing while at room temperature.

Divide the cabbage among 3 containers, add the beans and edamame, and drizzle with the remaining dressing.
To serve, add half an avocado, the cotija, cilantro and a last squeeze of lime. Eat cold.
#NOTE
Keeps 4–5 days and improves on day 2. Add a fried egg for about 6g more protein. Feta works in place of cotija.

@@ Saffron Risotto | Side | | | 6 | 40 min |
#ING
1 1/2 quarts chicken stock
Generous pinch of saffron threads
1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
1 1/2 cups carnaroli rice
1/2 cup dry white wine
4 tbsp cold unsalted butter, in chunks
1/2 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
Salt
#STEP
Warm half the stock with the saffron at a low simmer.
Soften the onion in the oil over medium-low, about 5 minutes. Add the rice and toast over medium-high 3–4 minutes.
Add the wine and stir until absorbed.
Add the saffron stock a ladle or two at a time, stirring as each is absorbed, then continue with the remaining stock until the rice is tender but not mushy and loose enough to cover the bottom of a bowl.
Beat in the butter and cheese vigorously, season with salt and serve immediately.
#NOTE
You can half-cook it, spread it out to cool, and finish it when guests arrive. Keep it soupy, not stiff.

@@ Sour Cherry Pie | Dessert | fave | | 8 | 2 hr 45 min + cooling |
#ING
2 recipes flaky all-butter pie dough (almond variation)
All-purpose flour, for rolling
8 cups pitted sour cherries (2.5 lb)
1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
5 tbsp cornstarch
2 tsp finely grated lemon zest
2 tsp vanilla extract
1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp cardamom, 1/4 tsp kosher salt
1/4 tsp almond extract
1 large egg, beaten
Demerara sugar, for the top
#STEP
Heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the lowest position.
Roll one dough to a 13-inch round, fit into a 9-inch pie plate with a 3/4-inch overhang and chill. Roll the second and cut into 1-inch strips; chill.
Toss the cherries with the sugar, cornstarch, zest, vanilla, spices, salt and almond extract.
Fill the crust, brush the edge with egg, and weave a lattice. Trim, fold the overhang in, crimp, brush with egg and sprinkle with demerara.
Bake on a foil-lined sheet 20 minutes, then at 350°F for 1 1/2 to 2 hours until bubbling in the center, tenting as needed.
Cool completely, at least 4 hours.
#NOTE
Freeze pitted cherries even if baking right away; they keep the pastry cold. Don't pull the pie until the center bubbles.

@@ Lasagna | Meal | fave | | 10 | 1 hr 30 min |
#ING
1 lb ground beef
1 lb Italian sausage
2 tsp dried basil
2 tsp dried oregano
1/2 tsp fennel seeds
2 1/2 tsp kosher salt, divided
1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
6 cups marinara sauce
1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
1 box no-boil lasagna noodles
16 oz ricotta cheese
1 egg
1/2 lb provolone, sliced
1/2 lb mozzarella, sliced
1 cup grated Parmesan
#STEP
Heat the oven to 375°F.
Brown the beef in a large pot in one piece, then break into chunks. Add the basil, oregano, 2 tsp salt, pepper flakes and fennel and toast until fragrant. Drain and add the marinara.
Mix the ricotta with the egg and 1/2 tsp salt.
Coat the noodles in the sauce. Spread 1 1/2 cups sauce in a 9 × 13 pan and add a layer of noodles.
Layer a third of the provolone, 1 1/2 cups sauce, a quarter of the mozzarella and Parmesan, and a third of the ricotta. Repeat.
Finish with noodles, then the remaining Parmesan and mozzarella. Cover with foil.
Bake 40 minutes, uncover and bake 10 more. Rest 20–25 minutes and garnish with parsley.
