@@ Poppy Seed Almond Cake | Dessert | | | 16 | 2 hr 15 min |
#ING
Neutral oil and flour, for the pan
2 1/3 cups (465 g) granulated sugar
2 tbsp poppy seeds
1 1/2 tsp baking powder
1 tsp kosher salt
3 cups (390 g) all-purpose flour
1 1/2 cups whole milk
1 1/2 cups neutral oil
3 large eggs
1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
1 1/2 tsp almond extract
Glaze: 3/4 cup powdered sugar, 1/4 cup orange juice, 2 tsp melted butter, 1/2 tsp vanilla, 1/2 tsp almond extract
#STEP
Heat the oven to 350°F. Oil and flour a 12-cup Bundt pan thoroughly.
Mix the sugar, poppy seeds, baking powder, salt and flour in a mixer.
Add the milk, oil, eggs and extracts, mix on low, then beat on medium-high 2 minutes until smooth and thick.
Bake 80–90 minutes until deep golden and a tester comes out clean. Cool 15 minutes, then invert onto a rack.
Poke holes all over the top. Whisk the glaze and brush it all over, re-brushing the drips. Cool completely.
#NOTE
Keeps 5 days wrapped. Two loaf pans work too, 75–80 minutes.

@@ Maple-Glazed Chickpea Bowl with Garlic Tahini Yogurt | Dinner | fave | | 4 | 45 min | ,19,,
#ING
2 cups dry farro (or 3 cups cooked)
1 1/3 tbsp avocado oil
20 oz canned chickpeas, drained, rinsed and very dry
1 1/3 shallots, minced
4 garlic cloves, grated (divided)
5/8 tsp ground coriander, 1/3 tsp fennel seeds, 5/8 tsp smoked paprika
4 tbsp maple syrup (divided)
2 5/8 tsp soy sauce or coconut aminos
1 1/3 tsp hot sauce
1 1/3 lemons, zested and juiced (divided)
1/3 cup fresh parsley, minced
4 tbsp tahini
4 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
1 1/3 tbsp water
1 1/3 English cucumbers, diced
1 1/3 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
2 5/8 cups baby arugula
2 5/8 oz feta, crumbled
1/3 cup pickled red onions
1 1/3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil and 1 1/3 tsp za'atar, to finish
#STEP

#STEP

@@ Universal Stock | Side | | | 24 | 8 hr |
#ING

4 lb chicken bones
2 tbsp tomato paste
2 tbsp all-purpose flour
2 large white onions, chopped
3 medium carrots, chopped
2 ribs celery, chopped
4 sprigs thyme, 4 sprigs parsley, 3 bay leaves
1 tsp black peppercorns
#STEP
Heat the oven to 400°F and oil 2 roasting pans.
Toss the bones with the tomato paste and flour and roast about 60 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned. Roast the vegetables in the other pan about 30 minutes.
