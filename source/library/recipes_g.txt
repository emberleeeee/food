@@ Overnight Apple Cinnamon Oats | Breakfast, Snack | fave | | 1 | 5 min + overnight |
#ING
1/3 cup rolled oats
1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
1 tbsp chia seeds
1 tsp agave syrup
1/4 small apple, finely diced
2 tbsp sliced almonds
1/4 tsp cinnamon
1/8 tsp vanilla extract
Pinch of salt
#STEP
Combine the oats, milk and chia in a jar.

@@ Apple Galette | Dessert | fave | | 8 | 1 hr 30 min |
#ING
1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
1 tbsp sugar
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
3–4 tbsp ice water
4 medium apples
3 tbsp sugar mixed with 2 tsp cinnamon
4 tbsp cold butter, in small cubes
1 egg, for egg wash
Vanilla ice cream, to serve
#STEP
Pulse the flour, sugar and salt, pulse in the butter to wet sand, then add ice water until a ball forms. Wrap and chill at least 30 minutes.
Heat the oven to 375°F. Roll the dough to a 12-inch circle on a lined tray and lightly press a 9-inch pan in the center as a guide.
Peel, core and slice the apples very thin, fan them within the circle, sprinkle with cinnamon sugar and dot with butter.
Fold the edge over the apples, brush with egg wash and bake 30–40 minutes until golden. Serve with ice cream.

@@ Mexican Corn Stew | Dinner, Slow cooker | | | 6 | 2 hr |
#ING
2 cans small red beans
1 tbsp olive oil
1 cup chopped onion
2 heaping tbsp minced garlic
1/4 cup diced jalapeños
1/2 cup thinly sliced carrots
1/2 cup thinly sliced celery
2 red bell peppers, diced
3 tbsp minced cilantro
6 cups water or vegetable stock
3 cups corn
2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp ground coriander
1/2 tsp cayenne (optional)
Crushed tortilla chips, salsa and nonfat yogurt, to serve
#STEP
Cook the onion and garlic in the oil over medium-high 3–5 minutes.
Add the jalapeños, carrots, celery and peppers for 3 minutes.
Add the cilantro, liquid, corn, spices and beans.
Cover and cook on low until thick, 1 1/2–2 hours on the stove or 6–8 hours in a slow cooker. Add cayenne to taste.
Top with crushed chips, salsa and yogurt.
#NOTE
Biggest upgrade: toast the cumin and coriander and caramelize a tablespoon of tomato paste with the onion before adding liquid. A chipotle in adobo, charred corn and a squeeze of lime at the end all help too.

@@ Roasted Chickpea & Kale Wrap | Meal | | | 1 | 20 min |
#ING
2/3 cup cooked chickpeas
1/2 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp soy sauce
1/2 tsp maple syrup
1/2 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp garlic powder, divided
2 tbsp mayonnaise
1 tbsp lemon juice
Pinch of salt
2–3 large kale leaves, chopped
1/4–1/2 avocado, sliced
2–3 tomato slices
2 heaping tbsp hummus
1 large tortilla
#STEP
Toss the chickpeas with the oil, soy, maple, paprika and 1/2 tsp garlic powder and air fry at 400°F for 10–11 minutes (15–18 in the oven).

Toast in a pan 1–2 minutes per side and halve.

@@ Double-Apple Crumble Cake | Dessert | | | 12 | 2 hr 15 min + cooling |
#ING
3 tbsp unsalted butter, plus more for the pan
4 medium Pink Lady apples, peeled, cored and sliced 1/4 inch thick
2 cups (260 g) all-purpose flour
1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
1 1/2 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp kosher salt
1 cup unsweetened apple butter
1 cup sugar
1/2 cup crème fraîche or sour cream
1/4 cup neutral oil
2 large eggs
2 tsp vanilla extract
1 batch crumble topping
#STEP
Cook the apples in the butter over medium, tossing, 10–15 minutes until slightly translucent. Cool.
Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9-inch springform and line the bottom with parchment.
Whisk the flour, cinnamon, leaveners and salt. Whisk the apple butter, sugar, crème fraîche, oil, eggs and vanilla.
Whisk wet into dry just until even, then fold in the apples, leaving the liquid behind.
Spread in the pan, top with crumble and bake 1 hour 20 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes until a tester comes out clean. Cool completely.
#NOTE
Keeps 4 days wrapped. Use apple butter that's just apples.

@@ Beef Ragù | Dinner | fave | | 8 | 4 hr |
#ING
2 1/2 lb beef chuck roast, in 2-inch chunks
2 tbsp olive oil
Salt and black pepper
1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
1 large yellow onion, finely diced
2 medium carrots, finely diced
2 celery stalks, finely diced
6 cloves garlic, minced
4 tbsp tomato paste
1 1/2 cups full-bodied red wine
1 (28-oz) can San Marzano crushed tomatoes
2–3 cups beef stock
2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
1 Parmesan rind (optional)
3 bay leaves, 4 thyme sprigs, 2 rosemary sprigs, 3 sage leaves
2 tbsp fresh basil, chopped
Pappardelle, Parmigiano-Reggiano and olive oil, to serve
#STEP
Pat the beef dry, season well and let it sit 30 minutes.
Sear in batches in the oil in a Dutch oven until deeply browned. Set aside and lower the heat.
Cook the onion, carrot and celery with a pinch of salt 15–20 minutes until caramelized. Add the garlic and pepper flakes, then the tomato paste until rust-colored.
Deglaze with the wine and reduce by half. Add the tomatoes, stock, Worcestershire, herbs, rind and beef.
Simmer partially covered on low 2 1/2–3 hours until fork-tender.
Shred the meat, discard the herbs and rind, and simmer uncovered 15–20 minutes. Season and stir in the basil.
Toss with pasta and a splash of pasta water; finish with cheese, olive oil and basil.
#NOTE
Better the next day. Keeps 3 days refrigerated or 3 months frozen.

@@ Spicy Sambal Beans & Rice | Dinner | fave | | 3 | 25 min |
#ING
1 cup jasmine or basmati rice (or 2 cups cooked)
1 tbsp neutral oil
1 small onion, finely chopped
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp sambal oelek
1 (15-oz) can black, kidney or navy beans, drained and rinsed
2 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
1 tsp brown sugar (optional)
Juice of 1/2 lime (or 1 tbsp rice vinegar)
Salt
Optional: 1 cup spinach or kale, 1/2 cup frozen peas or corn, a fried egg
#STEP
Cook the rice.
Soften the onion in the oil 3–5 minutes, then the garlic 30 seconds.
Bloom the sambal 1 minute, then add the beans, soy and sugar for 3–4 minutes. Wilt in any greens or vegetables.
Toss in the rice until coated and hot. Finish with lime and salt.
Top with a fried egg, scallions or extra sambal.
