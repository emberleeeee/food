@@ Chicken Curry | Dinner, Meal, Slow cooker | fave | | 6 | |
#ING
1 (14-oz) can Thai coconut milk
2 tsp fish sauce
2 oz Massaman curry paste (or red curry paste + 1 tbsp sugar)
2 tsp minced fresh ginger
Zest and juice of 1 lime, plus wedges for serving
1 stalk lemongrass
1 yellow onion, chopped
1/4 cup roasted unsalted peanuts, plus more for topping
1 lb small red potatoes, halved
2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
Kosher salt
Cooked white rice, for serving
Chopped fresh cilantro, for topping (optional)
#STEP
Whisk 1/2 cup water, the coconut milk, curry paste, fish sauce, ginger, onion and lime zest in a 6- to 8-quart slow cooker. Smash the lemongrass, tie it in a knot and add it with the peanuts and potatoes. Top with the chicken, press to submerge, cover and cook on LOW 7 hours.
Skim off some fat. Stir in the lime juice and season with salt. Break the chicken into chunks and let stand 5 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly; discard the lemongrass.
Serve with rice, topped with peanuts and cilantro, with lime wedges.

#ING

@@ Apple Cider Donuts | Dessert | fave | | 12 | |
#ING
2 cups apple cider, reduced to 1/4 cup
2 1/2 cups (300 g) all-purpose flour
1 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp salt
1 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, 1/4 tsp allspice
6 tbsp butter, browned and cooled slightly
2/3 cup (135 g) brown sugar
1/3 cup (65 g) granulated sugar
2 large eggs
1/4 cup sour cream
1/4 cup buttermilk
1 tsp vanilla
Coating: 6 tbsp butter, melted; 3/4 cup granulated sugar; 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
#STEP
Boil the cider down to 1/4 cup and let cool.
Whisk the dry ingredients together.
Whisk the brown butter and sugars, then add the eggs one at a time, then the sour cream, buttermilk, cooled cider and vanilla.
Fold wet into dry just until no streaks remain.
Pipe or spoon into a greased donut pan, 3/4 full. Bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes.
Cool 3–4 minutes, then while warm dip the tops in melted butter and then cinnamon sugar.
#NOTE

@@ Cinnamon Rolls | Dessert | fave | | 12 | 20 min prep, 35 min bake, 1 hr rise |
#ING
4 1/2–4 3/4 cups (600 g) bread flour, divided
1/3 cup (65 g) granulated sugar
2 packets instant yeast
1 1/3 cups (315 ml) milk
1/3 cup (75 g) salted butter, cubed
1 large egg, room temperature
Filling: 1/4 cup salted butter, softened; 3/4 cup brown sugar; 2 tbsp cinnamon
Frosting: 8 oz cream cheese; 1/2 cup salted butter; 2 cups powdered sugar; 1 tsp vanilla
#STEP
Whisk 2 cups flour with the sugar and yeast.
Microwave the milk and butter 45 seconds until warm, not hot. Add with the egg to the flour and mix. Add 2 more cups flour, then 1/2 cup, until a soft dough forms.
Knead until smooth and springy, adding up to 1/4 cup more flour if sticky. Rest 10 minutes, then roll to a 15 × 12-inch rectangle.
Spread with the softened butter, sprinkle with the cinnamon sugar, roll from the long side and cut into 12 with unflavored floss.
Place in a buttered 9 × 13 pan, cover and proof 1 hour.
Bake at 350°F for 30–35 minutes. Cool 10–15 minutes.
Beat the frosting ingredients together and spread on the warm rolls.
#NOTE
To freeze, wrap the unbaked, unrisen rolls tightly for up to 2 weeks; thaw and proof 1–2 hours before baking.

#ING
1 lb baby potatoes
1 (15-oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
1 lb Brussels sprouts, thinly shredded
2 shallots, thinly sliced
3 tbsp avocado oil
2 1/2 tsp garlic powder
2 1/2 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp ground coriander
1/3 cup fresh parsley, minced
Kosher salt and black pepper

@@ Goodfellas Prison Sauce | Meal | | | 8 | 3.5 hr |
#ING
2 tbsp olive oil, divided
1/2 lb beef chuck, cut into 1-inch pieces
1/2 lb veal, cut into 1-inch pieces
1/2 lb pork sausage, casing removed
1/2 cup yellow onion, chopped
6–8 cloves garlic, very thinly sliced
1/2 cup red or white wine
1/2 cup beef broth
2 (28-oz) cans Italian crushed tomatoes
1 (6-oz) can tomato paste
1/2 cup basil leaves, torn
Salt and black pepper
#STEP
Brown the beef and veal in batches in 1 tbsp oil in a heavy pot, 5–7 minutes per batch. Set aside.

@@ Rhubarb Cake | Dessert | | | 10 | 2 hr 15 min |
#ING
1 lb (454 g) rhubarb stalks
1/2 tsp baking soda
Butter and demerara sugar for the pan, plus demerara for the top
1 3/4 cups (228 g) all-purpose flour
2 1/2 tsp baking powder
3/4 tsp kosher salt
1 1/4 cups (250 g) granulated sugar
1 stick (113 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled
2 large eggs
1 1/2 tsp finely grated orange zest
1/3 cup (80 g) whole-milk Greek yogurt
1 tsp vanilla extract
#STEP
Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 4 1/2 × 8 1/2-inch loaf pan, line with a parchment sling, butter it and coat with demerara sugar.
Trim 4–5 thin 8 1/2-inch rhubarb stalks (about 4 oz) for the top. Chop the rest into 1/2-inch pieces.
Cook two-thirds of the chopped rhubarb (8 oz) with 1 tbsp water, mashing, until it looks like applesauce, 5–7 minutes. Cool completely, then stir in the baking soda.
Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt. In another bowl whisk the sugar, butter, eggs and zest until thick, then whisk in the yogurt, vanilla and rhubarb mush.
Whisk wet into dry just until combined, then fold in the reserved chopped rhubarb.
Scrape into the pan, lay the stalks on top and sprinkle generously with demerara.
Bake 60 minutes, then lower to 325°F and bake until a tester comes out clean, 80–90 minutes total. Tent with foil if it darkens.
Cool 20 minutes in the pan, lift out and cool completely.
#NOTE
Keeps up to 5 days well wrapped at room temperature. Err on the side of overbaking; it's very moist.

@@ Pistachio Pinwheels | Dessert | | | 32 | 2 hr 35 min |
#ING
2/3 cup (90 g) shelled pistachios
12 tbsp (170 g) unsalted butter, room temperature
3/4 cup + 2 tbsp (105 g) powdered sugar
2 large egg yolks
1/2 tsp almond extract
1 cup (130 g) all-purpose flour
1/2 tsp kosher salt
1 1/3 cups (160 g) almond flour
1/2 cup demerara sugar, for rolling
#STEP
Pulse the pistachios until very finely ground; set aside.
In the food processor, cream the butter and powdered sugar, add the yolks and almond extract, then pulse in the flour and salt to a stiff dough.
Work the almond flour into two-thirds of the dough. Roll it between parchment into a 12 × 8-inch slab, 1/4 inch thick, and chill until firm, 10–15 minutes.
Pulse the ground pistachios into the remaining dough until green.
Spread the pistachio dough over the almond slab, leaving a 1/2-inch border on the long sides. Roll into a tight log, wrap and chill at least 1 hour.
Heat the oven to 350°F. Roll the log firmly in demerara sugar, trim the ends and slice into 32 rounds.
Bake on two lined sheets 15–20 minutes until golden at the edges, rotating halfway. Cool on the sheets.
#NOTE
Keeps 5 days airtight or 2 months frozen. The unsliced log keeps 2 days refrigerated or 2 months frozen.
