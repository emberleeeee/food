@@ Salted Halvah Blondies | Dessert | | | 16 | 45 min + cooling |
#ING
Butter, for the pan
1 1/4 cups (163 g) all-purpose flour
1 tsp kosher salt
1/2 tsp baking powder
6 oz white chocolate, chopped
1 stick (113 g) unsalted butter
1/4 cup (70 g) tahini
1/2 cup (100 g) packed light brown sugar
1 large egg plus 2 yolks
1 tbsp vanilla extract
4 oz halvah, crumbled
2 tbsp sesame seeds
Flaky salt
#STEP
Heat the oven to 350°F and line an 8 × 8 pan with buttered foil.
Whisk the flour, salt and baking powder.
Melt the white chocolate, butter and tahini over simmering water until smooth; cool slightly.

Cool completely, lift out with the foil and cut into 16.

1/4 tsp salt, plus more to taste
3 tbsp white wine vinegar
1 tbsp cold water
