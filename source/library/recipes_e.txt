@@ Peanut Butter and Concord Grape Sandwich Cookies | Dessert | | | 30 | 3 hr 45 min |
#ING
2 1/2 cups (325 g) all-purpose flour
1/2 tsp baking soda
1 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more for the tops
2 sticks (227 g) unsalted butter, room temperature
1/2 cup (100 g) packed light brown sugar
1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar, plus more for the tops
1 cup (260 g) natural crunchy peanut butter
2 large eggs
1 tsp vanilla extract
Concord grape jam: 1 lb Concord grapes, 1/2 cup sugar, 2 tbsp lemon juice (or 1 1/2 cups store-bought jam)
#STEP
For the jam, squeeze the grape flesh into a saucepan and keep the skins. Simmer the flesh 5–10 minutes until pulpy, then press through a sieve onto the skins to remove the seeds. Return to the pan with the sugar and lemon, boil to 220°F (15–20 minutes), and chill until set.
Whisk the flour, baking soda and salt.
Beat the butter and sugars until light, about 2 minutes, then the peanut butter until creamy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the flour mixture and vanilla on low.
Roll the dough into two 2-inch logs, wrap and chill at least 2 hours.
Heat the oven to 350°F. Slice the logs into thin, even coins and space on lined sheets. Punch out the centers of half if making Linzers, and sprinkle those tops with sugar and a little salt.
Bake 13–15 minutes, rotating after 10. Cool completely.
Spread jam on the bottoms and press on the tops.
#NOTE
Best the day they're filled. Unfilled cookies keep 4 days; the jam keeps a month.

@@ Sesame Salmon with Farro & Garlic Edamame | Meal | fave | | 2 | 30 min | ,57,,
#ING
2 skin-on salmon fillets
2 tbsp soy sauce, plus 1 tsp for the edamame
1 tbsp toasted sesame oil, plus 1/2 tsp
1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
1 tbsp rice vinegar
3 garlic cloves, minced, divided
1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
1 tbsp sesame seeds
1 tbsp neutral oil, plus 1 tsp
2 scallions, sliced
1 cup dry farro
2 cups water or low-sodium chicken broth
1 1/2 cups frozen shelled edamame, thawed
Chili flakes (optional) and flaky salt
#STEP
Simmer the farro in the water or broth with a pinch of salt, covered, 25–30 minutes until tender. Drain.
Whisk the soy, sesame oil, honey, vinegar, 2 garlic cloves and ginger.
Pat the salmon dry, salt it, and sear flesh-side down in the neutral oil over medium-high 3–4 minutes.
Flip, pour over the glaze and cook 3–4 minutes, spooning it over until lacquered. Rest 2 minutes.
Blister the edamame in 1 tsp oil 2–3 minutes, add the remaining garlic for 30 seconds, then toss with 1 tsp soy and 1/2 tsp sesame oil. Finish with chili flakes and flaky salt.
Serve the salmon over farro with the edamame, sesame seeds and scallions.
#NOTE
Add 1 tsp gochujang to the glaze for a spicy-sweet version. With batch-cooked farro this comes together in 15 minutes.

@@ Rice Pudding Cake with Mango Caramel | Dessert | | | 10 | 1 hr 45 min + cooling |
#ING
Caramel: 1 cup sugar, 1 1/2 cups chopped mango, 1/2 cup heavy cream, 4 tbsp unsalted butter, 1/2 tsp kosher salt
1 cup Arborio or Carnaroli rice
3 cups whole milk
1 (12-oz) can evaporated milk
1 tsp kosher salt
3/4 cup sugar
1/4 cup dark rum
1/2 tsp ground cardamom
1 vanilla bean, seeds scraped, plus the pod
4 tbsp cold unsalted butter, plus more for the pan
2 large eggs plus 3 yolks
1 large mango, thinly sliced, to serve
#STEP
For the caramel, cook the sugar with 1/3 cup water to deep amber, add the mango and cook 5 minutes, then stream in the cream and whisk in the butter and salt. Simmer 5–10 minutes, then blend smooth.
Simmer the rice, milks, salt, sugar, rum, cardamom and vanilla, stirring often, 25–30 minutes until the rice is translucent and floats in the thickened liquid.
Stir in the butter and cool until warm.
Heat the oven to 350°F and butter and line a 10-inch cake pan.
Beat the eggs and yolks into the rice mixture, pour into the pan and bake 40–50 minutes until a tester comes out clean. Cool completely.
Unmold and serve slices with caramel and fresh mango.
#NOTE
Caramel keeps 1 week; the cake keeps 3 days refrigerated.

@@ Smashed Beef Kebab with Cucumber Yogurt | Meal | fave | | 4 | 25 min |
#ING
2 cups Greek yogurt
2 mini cucumbers, coarsely grated
3 tbsp finely chopped mint, plus leaves to serve
1 large garlic clove, finely grated
1 lb ground beef (80% lean) or lamb
1 small yellow onion, coarsely grated
1/2 tsp ground turmeric
2 tsp kosher salt, divided
Black pepper
1/4 cup walnuts
2 tbsp raisins or dried cranberries
Pomegranate molasses (optional)
Warm pita or white rice, to serve
#STEP
Mix the yogurt, cucumber, chopped mint and garlic and chill.
Mix the beef, onion, turmeric, 1 tsp salt and lots of pepper.

@@ Lentil-Chickpea Stew with Spinach | Dinner | | | 4 | 55 min |
#ING
1 cup dry lentils, rinsed
3 tbsp olive oil
3 cups diced onions
2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp paprika, 1/4 tsp ground allspice, 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
1/2 cup uncooked long-grain white or brown rice
6 1/2 cups water
2 bay leaves
1 1/2 tsp salt and black pepper
1 cup cooked chickpeas (or 1 can, rinsed)
3 cups fresh spinach
#STEP
Cook the onions in the oil over medium, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and jammy, 15–20 minutes.
Push the onions aside and toast the spices in the center 60 seconds.
Add the lentils, rice, water and bay leaves and bring to a boil.
Simmer partially covered 35–40 minutes with the salt and pepper until tender, adding water if it gets too thick.
Add the chickpeas and simmer 10 minutes.
Remove the bay leaves and wilt in the spinach. Season and serve with lemon or a drizzle of olive oil.
#NOTE
Caramelizing the onions is the flavor foundation. Freezes well; loosen leftovers with water.
