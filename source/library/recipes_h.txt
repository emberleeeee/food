@@ Sides and Sauces (Quick Set) | Side | | | 4 | 5–10 min each |
#ING
Lemon-yogurt sauce: 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, zest and juice of 1/2 lemon, 1 tbsp chopped dill, salt
Quick tzatziki: 1 cup Greek yogurt, 1/2 cucumber grated and squeezed dry, 1 garlic clove grated, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tbsp dill, salt

@@ Sausage Ricotta Hot Honey Pizza | Dinner | | https://cooktune.com/sausage-ricotta-honey-hot-pizza/ | 4 | 27 min | 390,18,36,20
#ING
1 ball pizza dough
1/2 cup pizza sauce or crushed San Marzano tomatoes
1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
1/2 cup cooked spicy Italian sausage, crumbled
1/2 cup ricotta cheese
1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
1 tbsp olive oil
Hot honey, for drizzling
Fresh basil leaves, to garnish (optional)
#STEP
Heat the oven to 475°F with a pizza stone or baking sheet inside.
Roll the dough out on a floured surface and move it to parchment.

Finish with a drizzle of hot honey and basil, then slice.
#NOTE
Makes one large pizza, about 8 slices; a serving is 2 slices.

@@ Rosemary and Cranberry Soda Bread | Snack | fave | | 10 | 1 hr |
#ING
450 g plain flour, plus extra for dusting
1 tsp baking soda
1 tsp salt
100 g dried cranberries
4 rosemary sprigs, leaves finely chopped
350 ml buttermilk, plus a little extra if needed
Butter, to serve
#STEP
Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
Sift the flour, baking soda and salt, stir in the cranberries and rosemary, and make a well.
Add the buttermilk and mix quickly into a soft dough, adding a splash more if needed.
Knead lightly, shape into a 6-inch round on a baking sheet and cut a deep cross on top.
Bake 15 minutes, then at 400°F for 20–25 more until golden and hollow-sounding underneath.
Cool 20 minutes and serve warm with butter.

@@ Hummus | Snack | | | 6 | 15 min |
#ING
240 g cooked chickpeas, rinsed well
60 g tahini, well stirred
30 g fresh lemon juice
30 g extra virgin olive oil
1 garlic clove
1/2 tsp ground cumin
3 g fine sea salt
45–75 g cold water
1 g baking soda (optional, for canned chickpeas)
#STEP
Optional for extra creaminess: simmer the chickpeas with water and the baking soda 5–7 minutes, drain, rinse and rub off loose skins.
Blend the tahini and lemon 1 minute until pale and fluffy.
Add the garlic, cumin and salt for 30 seconds, then the chickpeas for 1–2 minutes.
With the machine running, stream in the oil, then cold water a tablespoon at a time until smooth and light. Adjust to taste.
#NOTE
A serving is about 40 g. Blend in roasted red pepper, herbs or a spoonful of Greek yogurt for variations.

@@ Almond Butter Banana Bread | Dessert | | | 10 | 1 hr 35 min |
#ING
Coconut oil, for the pan
3 tbsp + 1/3 cup almond butter
1 tbsp + 2/3 cup sugar
1 tsp + 1/2 cup virgin coconut oil, melted
1 1/3 cups (173 g) all-purpose flour
1 tsp baking powder
1 tsp kosher salt
1/2 tsp baking soda
1/4 tsp ground cardamom
2 large eggs, cold
1 cup mashed very ripe banana
1/3 cup plain whole-milk Greek yogurt
1 tsp vanilla extract
1/2 cup roasted almonds, chopped (optional)
#STEP
Heat the oven to 350°F. Oil a 4 1/2 × 8 1/2 loaf pan and line with a parchment sling.
Stir 3 tbsp almond butter, 1 tbsp sugar and 1 tsp coconut oil into a swirl mixture.
Whisk the flour, baking powder, salt, baking soda and cardamom.
Whisk the eggs with the remaining sugar 30 seconds, then the banana, yogurt, vanilla, remaining almond butter and coconut oil.
Whisk into the dry ingredients until smooth, then fold in the almonds.
Pour into the pan, dollop the swirl mixture on top and drag figure eights through it.
Bake 60–70 minutes until a tester comes out clean. Cool 20 minutes, lift out and cool completely.
#NOTE
Keeps 4 days. Use truly overripe bananas.

@@ Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies | Dessert | | | 12 | 40 min |
#ING
1/2 cup (115 g) unsalted butter
1/2 cup (79 g) light brown sugar
1/3 cup (69 g) granulated sugar
1 large egg, room temperature
1 tbsp vanilla extract
1 1/4 cups (160 g) all-purpose flour
1/2 tsp baking soda
Pinch of salt
1 1/2 cups (225 g) semi-sweet chocolate chips
#STEP
Heat the oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet.
Brown the butter until deep amber and nutty, 5–7 minutes. Cool.
Whisk the flour, baking soda and salt.
Whisk the brown butter with both sugars 1–2 minutes, then the egg and vanilla 2–3 minutes.
Fold in the dry ingredients, then the chocolate chips.
Scoop onto the sheet with space between and bake 12 minutes until crisp at the edges and gooey in the center. Cool on the sheet.

@@ Stuffing | Side | fave | | 10 | 1 hr 15 min |
#ING
1 lb bread (white and sourdough or French), cubed and dried
1 cup unsalted butter
2 medium onions, finely chopped
3–4 celery stalks, finely chopped
4 garlic cloves, minced
2–3 cups chicken or turkey stock
1/4 cup fresh parsley, 1 tbsp fresh sage, 1 tbsp fresh thyme, 1 tbsp fresh rosemary
1/2 tsp dried marjoram (optional)
1 tsp kosher salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper
2 large eggs, beaten
1/2 cup dried cranberries (optional)
1/2 cup toasted pecans or walnuts (optional)
#STEP
Dry the bread cubes in a 300°F oven 10–15 minutes if not stale.
Soften the onions and celery in the butter 8–10 minutes, add the garlic for 1 minute, then the herbs, salt and pepper for 1 minute.
Toss with the bread, plus cranberries and nuts if using.
Moisten with 2 cups stock, adding more 1/4 cup at a time if dry, then stir in the eggs.
Bake in a greased 9 × 13 at 350°F covered 30 minutes, then uncovered 20–25 minutes until golden.

@@ Marcona Almond Cookies | Dessert | | | 24 | 30 min |
#ING
4 oz Marcona almonds, plus about 24 whole for topping
1 (7-oz) tube almond paste, crumbled
1/4 cup sugar
1 tsp vanilla extract
2 large eggs (1 whole, 1 separated)
#STEP
Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet.
Pulse the almonds until coarse, then add the almond paste, sugar, vanilla, the whole egg and one egg white and process until smooth. Beat the yolk with 2 tsp water for egg wash.
Pipe or scoop 1 1/2-inch rounds onto the sheet, brush with egg wash and press an almond into each.
Bake 10–12 minutes until puffed and golden. Cool completely before peeling off.
#NOTE
Keeps 5 days or 2 months frozen. Salted roasted cashews work if you can't find Marconas.

@@ Key Lime Pie Bars | Dessert | | | 24 | 1 hr + cooling |
#ING
2 cups graham cracker crumbs
1/2 cup brown sugar
1/4 cup melted butter
42 oz (3 cans) sweetened condensed milk
12 egg yolks
2 cups key lime juice
4 tbsp fresh lime zest, divided
2 cups heavy cream
2 tbsp sugar
2 tsp vanilla extract
#STEP
Heat the oven to 300°F. Mix the crumbs, brown sugar and butter, press into a 9 × 13 pan and bake 15 minutes. Cool.
Whisk the condensed milk, yolks, lime juice and 2 tbsp zest, pour into the crust and bake 25–30 minutes until set with a slight jiggle. Cool completely.
Whip the cream with the sugar and vanilla to soft peaks, spread over the top and sprinkle with the remaining zest.

@@ Mayonnaise | Side | | | 16 | 15 min |
#ING
2 egg yolks
1 1/2 tbsp white wine vinegar, divided
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp dry mustard
1 3/4 cups vegetable, canola or grapeseed oil
