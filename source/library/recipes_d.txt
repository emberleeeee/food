@@ Turkey Kofta with Roasted Cauliflower | Dinner | | | 2 | 35 min | 540,42,,
#ING
1 lb 93% lean ground turkey
1/4 onion, grated
2 cloves garlic, grated
1/4 cup parsley, chopped, plus 1 cup for the salad
1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp cinnamon
1 tsp salt
1/2 head cauliflower, florets
1 tbsp olive oil
3/4 cup tzatziki
Parsley salad: parsley, mint, lemon, olive oil, salt
#STEP
Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the cauliflower with the oil and salt and roast 25 minutes.
Mix the turkey, onion, garlic, 1/4 cup parsley, spices and salt and shape into 8 oval logs.
Roast the kofta next to the cauliflower 15–18 minutes to 165°F, or grill.
Toss the parsley and mint with lemon, oil and salt.
Serve the kofta with cauliflower, tzatziki and the herb salad.

@@ Stuffed Peppers | Meal | fave | | 4 | 1 hr | 385,38,34,12
#ING
4 large bell peppers
1 lb ground chicken or turkey (93% lean)
1 cup cooked farro
1 (15-oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
1 (14-oz) can diced tomatoes, drained
1 small yellow onion, diced
3 garlic cloves, minced
1 tbsp olive oil
1 1/2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
3/4 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper
3/4 cup shredded Mexican blend or sharp cheddar
2 tbsp cilantro or scallions, to garnish
#STEP
Heat the oven to 400°F and grease a baking dish.
Cut the tops off the peppers, remove the seeds and stand them upright, trimming the bottoms if needed.
Soften the onion in the oil 4–5 minutes, then add the garlic for 60 seconds.
Add the meat and cook over medium-high 6–7 minutes, letting it brown before stirring.
Add the spices, salt and pepper, then the tomatoes for 2 minutes. Off the heat, fold in the farro and beans.
Pack the filling into the peppers and top with cheese. Add 1/4 cup water to the dish, cover with foil and bake 30 minutes.
Uncover and bake 10 more minutes. Rest 5 minutes and garnish.
#NOTE
Make the filling ahead and bake fresh; stuffed peppers keep 4 days. For a heartier plate, serve over more farro. Swap 1 tbsp gochujang for the chili powder for a Korean-style version.

@@ Sunday Gravy | Dinner | | | 6 | 4 hr |
#ING
2 1/2 lb oxtail, cut into pieces
Salt and pepper
2 tbsp olive oil
2–3 lb pork neck bones
2 lb sweet or hot pork sausage links
1 large onion, finely chopped
5 garlic cloves, crushed
3 tbsp tomato paste
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp red pepper flakes
2 cups dry red wine
2 cups dark stock
2 (28-oz) cans crushed tomatoes
2 bay leaves
2 sprigs thyme or rosemary and 1 sprig basil, tied in cheesecloth
1 lb dry rigatoni
4–6 oz Parmigiano-Reggiano, for grating
#STEP
Heat the oven to 350°F. Season the oxtail and brown it and the neck bones in batches in the oil in a Dutch oven; set aside. Brown the sausages and set aside.
Cook the onion in the pot, scraping up the browned bits. Add the garlic for 1 minute, then the tomato paste, oregano and pepper flakes until the paste darkens.
Deglaze with the wine and reduce by half. Add the stock, tomatoes, bay leaves and herb bundle, return the oxtail and bones, season and bring to a boil.
Cover and braise in the oven about 2 1/2 hours. Add the sausages and braise 30 minutes more.
Discard the herbs. Cook the rigatoni al dente, dry it briefly in the hot pot, and toss with enough sauce to coat.
Serve the pasta and sausages with extra sauce and grated cheese.

@@ Cheesy Potato & Red Onion Croquettes | Side, Snack | fave | | 20 | 1 hr |
#ING
2 lb Russet or Yukon Gold potatoes
1 medium red onion, finely diced
1–1 1/2 cups shredded cheese
2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
1/2–1 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
1 tbsp butter or olive oil
1 cup flour
2 eggs, beaten
1 1/2 cups breadcrumbs or panko
#STEP
Boil the peeled, chopped potatoes in salted water until very tender, 12–15 minutes. Drain, let the steam escape, and mash.
Sauté the onion in the butter 5–8 minutes until lightly caramelized, adding the garlic at the end.
Mix the potatoes, onion, cheese and seasonings and chill 15–20 minutes.
Roll 2-tbsp portions into logs and coat in flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
Air fry at 400°F for 10–15 minutes or bake at 425°F for 15–20 minutes, flipping halfway.
#NOTE
Freeze breaded croquettes on a tray, then bag them; they keep 3 months and cook from frozen.

@@ Brown Sugar Cinnamon Pop Tarts | Dessert | | | 8 | 1 hr 30 min |
#ING
2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1 tbsp sugar (optional)
1 tsp fine salt
1 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
1/4–1/2 cup cold water
1 egg, for egg wash
Filling: 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1 tbsp flour, 2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp salt, 2 tbsp softened butter, 1 tbsp maple syrup (optional)
Glaze (optional): 1/2 cup powdered sugar, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 2–3 tbsp milk, 1/2 tsp vanilla, pinch of cinnamon

@@ Strawberry Pop Tarts | Dessert | | | 9 | 1 hr 30 min |
#ING
2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1 tbsp sugar
1 tsp salt
1 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
4–6 tbsp ice water
1 cup strawberry preserves
1 tbsp cornstarch
1 tsp lemon juice
Glaze: 1 cup powdered sugar, 2–3 tbsp milk, 1/2 tsp vanilla, pink food coloring, rainbow sprinkles
#STEP
Pulse the flour, sugar and salt, pulse in the butter to coarse crumbs, then add ice water until it just comes together. Halve, wrap and chill 30 minutes.
Mix the preserves with the cornstarch and lemon juice.
Roll each half to 9 × 12 inches and cut nine 3 × 4-inch pieces. Add 1 tbsp filling to half, brush the edges with water, top, seal with a fork and vent.
Chill 15 minutes, then bake at 375°F for 20–25 minutes. Cool completely.
Spread with the glaze, add sprinkles and let set 1 hour.
