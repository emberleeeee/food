@@ Cilantro Lime Bean Salad | Dinner, Meal | fave | | 6 | 30 min |
#ING
Pickled jalapeños: 2 tbsp white wine vinegar, 1/2 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, 1 jalapeño thinly sliced, 2 tbsp cilantro minced
1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
Zest and juice of 2 limes
1 English cucumber
2 tbsp olive oil
4 cloves garlic, crushed
1 jalapeño, roughly chopped
1 tbsp white wine vinegar
2 tsp agave syrup
1 tsp yellow miso paste
2/3 cup fresh cilantro leaves, divided
1 (15-oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
1 (15-oz) can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
Kosher salt
2 avocados, to serve
6 tostadas, to serve
#STEP
Whisk the vinegar, sugar and salt until dissolved, add the sliced jalapeño and cilantro, and set aside.
Massage the red onion with the zest and juice of 1 lime and a pinch of salt until it softens.
Halve the cucumber, smash it with the flat of a knife, cut into 1-inch pieces, salt and let drain 10 minutes, then squeeze out the water.
Warm the oil over medium-low and cook the garlic until golden, 2–3 minutes.
Blend the garlic oil with the chopped jalapeño, the second lime's zest and juice, vinegar, agave, miso, 1/3 cup cilantro and salt until smooth.
Toss the onions, beans, cucumber and remaining cilantro with the dressing.
Spread salted mashed avocado on tostadas, top with bean salad and pickled jalapeños.

@@ Banana Nut Chia Pudding | Breakfast | fave | https://plantbasedrdblog.com/2024/02/banana-nut-chia-pudding/ | 3 | 10 min + 1 hr chill |
#ING
1 banana, plus extra slices to serve
3/4 cup unsweetened soy or other plant milk
1/3 cup unsweetened plant-based yogurt
2 tbsp maple syrup
2 tbsp raw pecans
1 tsp vanilla
1 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp cardamom
1/3 cup chia seeds
Crumble: 2 medjool dates, 1/3 cup pecans, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, pinch of salt
#STEP
Blend the banana, milk, yogurt, maple, pecans, vanilla, spices and a pinch of salt until smooth.
Whisk into the chia seeds, rest 5 minutes, whisk again, cover and chill at least an hour.
Pulse the crumble ingredients until crumbly, without turning it to paste.
Layer crumble, pudding and more crumble, and top with banana.
#NOTE
Blend in protein powder (plus 1/4–1/2 cup extra milk) for more protein.

@@ Bourbon BBQ Bacon Cheeseburger Meatball Subs | Dinner |  | https://timeofrecipes.com/bourbon-bbq-bacon-cheeseburger-meatball-subs/ | 4 | 40 min | 745,36,58,42
#ING
1 lb ground beef (80/20)
1 cup breadcrumbs
1 egg
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
1 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp black pepper
1 tbsp olive oil
1 cup bourbon BBQ sauce
1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
4 sub rolls
8 slices crispy bacon
#STEP
Preheat oven to 375F.
Mix beef, breadcrumbs, egg, and seasonings just until combined, then roll into 16-20 meatballs about 1.5 inches wide.
Heat oil in a large skillet over medium and brown the meatballs on all sides in a single layer, 5-7 minutes, working in batches if needed.
Put all meatballs back in the pan, add the BBQ sauce, reduce heat, and simmer 10 minutes, stirring gently.
Meanwhile, split the rolls lengthwise without cutting through and toast them on a baking sheet in the oven about 5 minutes.
Fill each roll with 4-5 meatballs plus extra sauce, then top with 1/4 cup cheddar and 2 slices bacon.
Bake 2-3 minutes more until the cheese melts and serve immediately.
#NOTE
Meatballs can be made ahead and frozen raw or cooked for up to 3 months. Let them sear before turning so they hold together.

@@ Green Goddess Smashed Potato Salad | Dinner, Meal |  | https://plantbasedrdblog.com/2024/05/green-goddess-smashed-potato-salad/ | 3 | 40 min |
#ING
1 lb baby Yukon potatoes
1 head broccoli, cut into florets
1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
2 1/2 tbsp oil
Kosher salt
1 tbsp nutritional yeast
2 tbsp fresh dill, minced
1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/2 tsp ground coriander
1/2 tsp thyme
2 cups shredded lacinato kale
2 cups shredded romaine lettuce
Pickled red onions, for serving
Dressing: 1 cup unsweetened plain plant-based yogurt
Dressing: 1 garlic clove, grated
Dressing: zest and juice of 1 lime
Dressing: 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
Dressing: 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
Dressing: 1/2 cup fresh dill, woody stems removed
Dressing: 1/2 cup fresh parsley, woody stems removed
Dressing: 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
Dressing: 1/4 tsp kosher salt, or more as needed
#STEP
Boil the potatoes in well-salted water about 10 minutes, then cool.
Blend all dressing ingredients with an immersion blender until smooth and adjust salt.
Dry the rinsed chickpeas thoroughly with a towel.
Heat oven to 425F. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp oil, the nutritional yeast, and salt, place on half of a baking sheet, and gently smash.
Toss chickpeas with 1 tbsp oil and salt, spread on the other half of the sheet, and roast on the lower rack 20 minutes.
Scatter the minced dill and 1 tsp garlic powder over the tray, toss, and roast 10-15 minutes more.
Toss broccoli with the remaining oil, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, coriander, thyme, and salt, and roast cut side down on the middle rack 20-25 minutes until browned underneath; chop roughly.
Massage kale with 2 tbsp dressing and a pinch of salt, then add romaine, broccoli, potatoes, and 1/2 cup dressing and toss.
Divide among plates and finish with chickpeas, pickled onions, and more dressing.
#NOTE
Potatoes, dressing, and dried chickpeas can all be prepped ahead and refrigerated. Get the chickpeas as dry as possible so they crisp.

@@ Yuca-Vegetable Chili | Dinner | | | 4 | 1 hr | 356,8,68,5
#ING
1 1/4 lb yuca, peeled, in 1-inch chunks
1/2 lime
1 onion, chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
1/2 red or green bell pepper, chopped
2 tbsp margarine or butter
1 (16-oz) can chickpeas, with liquid
6 ripe tomatoes, peeled, seeded and chopped
2 scallions, chopped
Fresh cilantro (optional)
1/2 cup grated cheddar (optional)
#STEP
Simmer the yuca in salted water with the lime juice until tender, 35–40 minutes.
Meanwhile cook the onion, garlic, jalapeño and pepper in the margarine over medium.
Add the chickpeas with their liquid and the tomatoes and cook 3 minutes.
Drain the yuca, add it, and cook about 10 minutes until the flavors blend.
Top with scallions, cilantro and cheese.
#NOTE
Low in protein as written; doubling the chickpeas or adding Greek yogurt on top helps.

@@ Baked Apple Cider Donuts (Triple Batch) | Dessert | | https://mikebakesnyc.com/recipes/baked-apple-cider-donuts/ | 27 | 40 min (plus about 40 min to reduce the cider) | 197,2,33,6
#ING
1125 g (about 4 3/4 cups) apple cider
180 g (about 1 1/2 sticks plus 1 tbsp) unsalted butter
150 g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
3 large eggs
90 g (about 1/3 cup) sour cream
1 tbsp vanilla extract
375 g (about 3 cups) all-purpose flour
1 1/2 tsp baking powder
1 1/2 tsp baking soda
1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
3/4 tsp ground nutmeg
Heaping 1/4 tsp ground cloves
Heaping 1/4 tsp coarse sea salt
Topping: 300 g (1 1/2 cups) granulated sugar
Topping: 1 tbsp ground cinnamon, 1 1/2 tsp apple pie spice
About 3/4 cup reserved cider-butter mixture, to top
Cooking spray, for the pan
#STEP
Simmer the cider over low heat, stirring now and then, until it's down to about 2 1/4 cups. A triple batch takes longer than the original's 20 minutes, so use your widest pot and start checking at 25 minutes. Stir in the butter until melted and let cool 10 minutes.
Heat the oven to 350°F and spray the donut pan.
Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves and salt in a large bowl.
In another bowl, whisk the sugar, eggs, sour cream, vanilla and 1 1/2 cups of the cider-butter mixture. Keep the rest (about 3/4 cup) for the topping.
Fold the wet mix into the dry just until combined; the batter will be fairly thick.
Pipe the batter from a zip-top or pastry bag into the pan, filling each cavity about halfway.
Bake 9 to 10 minutes, until lightly browned and springy when pressed. Cool 2 minutes in the pan, then move to a rack. Re-spray and repeat until the batter is gone (27 donuts, so several rounds).
Stir the topping sugar, cinnamon and apple pie spice together. Dip each warm donut on both sides in the reserved cider-butter, then toss in the spiced sugar.
#NOTE
Tripled from Mike Bakes NYC's 9-donut recipe. Macros are per donut and come from the source. Best the day they're made; keep leftovers covered at room temperature up to 2 days.
